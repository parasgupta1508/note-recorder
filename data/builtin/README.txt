Built-in pieces for Piece practice. index.json lists them; the app installs each one into the Pieces library on the device the first time it opens (once: a piece the player deletes is not put back).
canon-in-d-violin-solo.mxl: Pachelbel's Canon in D, solo violin arrangement. Added by the app's owner, who confirmed it may be shared. Pachelbel's composition is public domain.
