What it has learned

Players

Each player has their own scores and progress. Names are typed here and stay on this device only.

Instrument

Choose what you play. The Tuner, Practice, Recorder and pitch range all switch to match, so you don't have to set them one by one.

Tuning

What counts as “in tune”.

A4 is the pitch of the A above middle C. 440 is standard, 442 is common in orchestras, 415 is baroque.

Most people cannot hear less than 3–5¢. 100¢ is one semitone.

Microphone

Watch the Input bar at the top while you play: it turns green when your sound is loud enough to be analysed.

Takes 7 seconds. Sets the noise gate and input level for your room.
Manual levels

Gate: sound quieter than the white tick is ignored. Raise it if the tuner reacts to room noise. Boost: raise it if your microphone is quiet; lower it if the bar shows CLIPPING.

Mic processing: turn the microphone on to check.

Detection

How careful the app is before it shows a note. Pick a preset, or fine-tune below.

The Recorder's Fast tempo mode uses its own values for these two.

Pitch range

Tap your instrument, or type your own limits. A narrower range avoids octave mistakes and saves battery. The Tuner already narrows it to your instrument's strings; this applies to the Recorder and Practice.

Engines

Which method listens and writes the notes. “Auto” keeps today's behaviour. The Detection presets above stay where they are.

Accuracy check

The software test proves the maths. The live check measures your own microphone and instrument.

Live check: tap, then play one steady note for 4 seconds (turn the microphone on first).

Backup & restore

Saves every player's scores and history in one file. Send it to yourself by WhatsApp or email, or keep the downloaded copy. A backup can only be imported if its version number is higher than the data on this device.

Settings layout

How this page is arranged.

Install app

Install the app on this device so it opens like a normal app, works without internet, and keeps your recordings' analysis files.

Developer

Tools for checking how the app listens. You do not need them to practise.

Reset

Put every setting above back to its recommended value.

Who is practising?

Type a name. It stays on this device and is never sent anywhere. You can add more players later in Settings.
