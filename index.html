Tuning

How notes are judged.

±10¢ suits learners. Most tuners use ±5¢; people rarely hear less than 3–5¢. Try 5 when scores are all green.

Detection

Advanced. The defaults suit most instruments.

Higher confidence = fewer wrong readings but slower to react. Narrow the pitch range to your instrument (for example 150–1200) to avoid octave jumps and save battery.

Accuracy check

Feeds generated tones (clean, noisy and quiet, with random detune) through the detector and shows the worst error for each. Press again for a fresh run.

Backup & restore

Saves both children's scores and history in one file. Send it to yourself by WhatsApp or email, or keep the downloaded copy. A backup can only be imported if its version number is higher than the data on this device.
